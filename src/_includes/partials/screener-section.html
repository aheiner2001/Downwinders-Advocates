The covered lists are specific and short. Anyone who tells you "any cancer counts" is wrong. Open the list below if you are not sure.

The 19 cancers covered for downwinders

Leukemia other than chronic lymphocytic leukemia, multiple myeloma, lymphomas other than Hodgkin's disease, and primary cancer of the thyroid, male or female breast, esophagus, stomach, pharynx, small intestine, pancreas, bile ducts, gall bladder, salivary gland, urinary bladder, brain, colon, ovary, liver (except where cirrhosis or hepatitis B is indicated), or lung.

Uranium miners, millers and ore transporters have a different list of seven: lung cancer, pulmonary fibrosis, cor pulmonale related to fibrosis of the lung, silicosis, pneumoconiosis, renal cancer, and other chronic renal disease including nephritis and kidney tubal tissue injury. Four of those seven are not cancer.

Manhattan Project waste claims use a third list of 21, which adds bone cancer and renal cancer, and the illness must have begun at least two years after exposure.
